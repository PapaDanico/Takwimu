# Research products for Siaya and for Kenya's 47 counties (draft, 28 Nov 2026)

Status: options for the owner, not a plan of record. Nothing here is on sale. Prices are not proposed.

**Principle (owner).** People pay for verified analysis, synthesis, monitoring and intelligence made to their question. They do not pay for public documents in a new cover. Every public figure stays free on takwimukenya.com. A paid product sells the work: reconciling sources, checking pages a second time, explaining conflicts, tracking changes, and answering the client's question. It never sells access to a number.

**Hard limits on every product.** Sales are manual through the Danico Ventures WhatsApp link. The site has no accounts, payments, forms, cookies or analytics, and no personal data is held, so there is no ODPC engagement (`docs/data-protection-policy.md`). Nothing goes into a paid product until an independent reviewer has approved it, with a hash-matched record in `data/reviews.json`. Source licences apply, and so do the no-verdict rule for named individuals and the withholding of contractor names.

## 3. Cross-cutting

**Shared pipeline (why each county gets cheaper).** The steps are: `lib/county-registry.js` (new counties go in `data/counties/<slug>/`); `source-library.json` plus `source-manifest.json` (SHA-256); OCR with table, PDF page and printed page; machine second reading (`machine-checks.json`); conflict register; review packs; `reviews.json` with `test-reviews.js` refusing "Approved" without a hash-matched record; `test-unpublished-counties.js` keeping unreviewed counties out of `dist`. The 47-county CoB layer already exists for every county. Kisumu's plan puts full Siaya parity at about 42 agent-days and the minimum threshold at 22-25 days, plus human review (`kisumu-plan.md` §3). Paid products should use only the reviewed subset. That makes review hours, not collection, the unit cost to track.

**Sequencing.**
1. **Settle licences and review first.** Land the `commercial_reuse` field, resolve the KNBS/KDHS discrepancy, then appoint a reviewer and clear the 80 machine-check disagreements.
2. **First build: the pending-bills reconciliation pack, Siaya in 47-county context (S1 + N1).** It uses the most data already held (7 Siaya conflicts, 4 FYs × 47 counties, sums that check). There is a live public dispute over the national figure. Review effort is bounded (Siaya ledger cases plus about 190 national cells). It serves an oversight buyer without judging any person.
3. Then N2 peer packs and S2 audit trail, which reuse the same reviewed cells.
4. Then S3 partner atlas and N3 OSR monitor. Defer S6, S7, N5 and N8 until collection fills their gaps.

**Manual pilot.**
- *Sample pack:* the first build above. It is a free, clearly marked sample, with figures limited to reviewed cells, the method and conflict appendix, and a one-page "what we would monitor for you" offer.
- *Target institutions (8, by type; contact through public channels only):* (1) Siaya County Assembly budget committee; (2) Senate Standing Committee on Finance and Budget secretariat; (3) Council of Governors finance committee secretariat; (4) Parliamentary Budget Office; (5) IBP Kenya / Bajeti Hub; (6) Okoa Uchumi or the National Taxpayers Association; (7) one audit or consulting firm with public-sector work; (8) one development partner programme on county PFM or FLLoCA. Include CRA if N6 is tested. Records stay at organisation level only, as in `docs/commercial/interview-guide.md`.
- *Feedback questions:* Which figure did you not already have, or could not reconcile yourself? Did the conflict explanation change a decision or a question you would ask? What would you need monitored, and how often? Who approves a purchase like this, and through what procurement route? Would a county-government client paying us reduce your trust? What would make you cite it publicly?

**Pricing options (for the owner to choose; no numbers proposed).** (a) Per pack, fixed scope. (b) Retainer for monitoring (quarterly or monthly digest). (c) Day rate for bespoke questions, scope agreed in writing. (d) Institutional licence for reviewed, versioned datasets, limited to sources whose terms allow it. (e) Tiered terms: lower for civil society and assemblies, standard for firms and partners, applied by organisation type and published. (f) Sponsored free public release, funded by a grant with no editorial rights. The test price ranges in `docs/commercial/pilot-one-pagers.md` remain hypotheses.

**Risks.**
- *Credibility:* OCR errors (80 disagreements) and 0 approved records. One wrong number in a paid pack damages the free site too. Mitigation: reviewed cells only, a public corrections log (`corrections.json`, 7 entries), and the same correction applied to paid and free outputs.
- *Licence:* 6 "permission required" sources, NC terms on KNBS GCP (and possibly KDHS), county copyright on 20 Siaya documents. Never redistribute documents (only 5 of 242 may be). Sell analysis and cite by link.
- *Neutrality:* N7 and S5 touch elected offices. Use one product and one price for every party, publish the method, give no ranking of individuals, and do no campaign work.
- *Conflicts of interest:* a county government or assembly buying a pack about itself. Disclose clients by type in a public register, give clients no right of review over findings, and never let paid work change a published figure. Decide in advance whether Takwimu will accept the Siaya County Executive as a client while it monitors that executive.
- *Competition:* PBO, CoB and IBP outputs are free. If the product does not reconcile or monitor, it has no reason to exist.
