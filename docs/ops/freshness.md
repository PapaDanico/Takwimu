## Cadence basis

| Rule | Publisher | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| wdi | World Bank WDI | Annual values; WDI is refreshed several times a year (World Bank data release calendar). Lag of 18 months after year end is a working assumption. |
| wdi-irregular | World Bank WDI | Survey-based indicator (household, Findex or school surveys): no fixed cadence; check the WDI release notes. |
| cob | Controller of Budget | County Budget Implementation Review Reports: quarterly and annual (PFM Act s.39 and Controller of Budget Act). Datasets here use the annual report; lag of 9 months after 30 June is a working assumption based on past release dates. |
| internal | Takwimu (compiled or calculated) | Rebuilt from other datasets at every release; freshness follows its inputs. |
| composite | Several publishers | Built from documents with different cadences (surveys, plans, reports); assess each source in docs/ops/source-health.md and the dataset notes. |
| knbs-gcp | KNBS (Gross County Product) | Gross County Product report, annual since 2019; each edition covers to the previous calendar year (GCP 2025 covers 2024). |
| ibp | International Budget Partnership | Open Budget Survey and County Budget Transparency Survey: biennial rounds (IBP). Lag of 9 months after the round year is a working assumption. |
| oag | Office of the Auditor-General | Annual audit of each county entity; Constitution Art. 229(4) and Public Audit Act 2015: report within six months after the end of the financial year. |
| iebc | IEBC | General election every five years (Constitution Art. 101; next on the second Tuesday of August 2027). Voter register and candidate counts follow each election. |
| static | American Red Cross (HDX) | One-off boundary layer (2016 vintage); changes only when IEBC gazettes new boundaries. |
| cbk-monthly | Central Bank of Kenya | Monthly statistical series on centralbank.go.ke (Statistics pages). Lag of 2 months is a working assumption; public debt and fiscal tables can lag longer. |
| cbk-annual | Central Bank of Kenya / KNBS | Annual GDP from the KNBS Economic Survey (published each April to May). Lag of 6 months after year end. |
| cbk-daily | Central Bank of Kenya | Weekly Bulletin (CBK, published every Friday). Lag of 14 days is a working assumption. |
| cra | Commission on Revenue Allocation | Annual recommendation on revenue sharing for the coming financial year (Constitution Art. 216, PFM Act s.190), released ahead of the year it covers. |
