# Takwimu Kenya pilot products (draft, 21 Nov 2026)
*Turning Data into Progress.* Prices are test hypotheses from the master handover (section 10), not demonstrated willingness to pay. Delivered as files; no accounts, logins or stored client data (docs/data-protection-policy.md). Requests by WhatsApp to Danico Ventures Ltd.

Common terms for every product: built only from published, aggregate public data; every figure carries its source, page, period and confidence; unreviewed figures are marked provisional; the same offer is open to every party, office and organisation; paid work never changes a published figure; no campaign canvassing, voter targeting or direct-marketing work.

## 1. Siaya Evidence Pack (quarterly)
**For:** NGOs, development partners, consultancies, county oversight researchers.
**What you get:** a PDF brief and CSV pack: fiscal execution and reconciliation updates, new audit findings, sector indicators that changed, new source documents, and a change log since the last pack.
**Test price:** KES 15,000 to 35,000 per pack.
**Delivery:** within 10 working days of each quarter's Controller of Budget report.

## 2. Bespoke research brief
**For:** organisations planning a programme, investment or review in Siaya.
**What you get:** a scoped question (for example "health financing and service delivery in Rarieda, FY2020/21 to FY2024/25"), a written brief with source appendix and calculation IDs, and one revision round.
**Test price:** KES 75,000 to 150,000 depending on scope; scope and price agreed in writing before work starts.

## 3. Monitoring digest (monthly)
**For:** teams that track county finance or audits.
**What you get:** a monthly PDF and CSV digest of new reports, audit items and data changes for the topics agreed. Sent as a file to the client's chosen channel; no platform account.
**Test price:** KES 25,000 to 50,000 per month.

## 4. Data licence
**For:** researchers and media needing bulk, versioned data.
**What you get:** the normalised datasets with documentation and version stamps. Third-party documents are linked, never resold; each dataset's source licence applies.
**Price:** negotiated.

## 5. Training
**For:** professional teams.
**What you get:** a half-day or full-day session on reading county budgets, audit reports and data provenance, using Siaya as the worked example.
**Price:** day rate, agreed in writing.
