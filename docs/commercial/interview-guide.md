# Customer discovery interview guide (draft, 21 Nov 2026)
Goal: 10 to 15 conversations (handover section 10) with NGOs, consultancies, county oversight researchers, development agencies, suppliers and universities, to test whether the pilot products solve a real problem and at what price.

**Data rule (no ODPC engagement):** record notes at organisation level only (organisation type, sector, county focus). Do not record names, phone numbers, emails or anything that identifies the person. Do not share notes outside Danico Ventures. Do not imply endorsement by any interviewee or their organisation.

## Opening (2 minutes)
We are building Takwimu Kenya, a public, source-linked evidence platform, starting with Siaya. We are not selling today; we want to understand how you work with county data.

## Current workflow (10 minutes)
1. When did you last need county figures (budget, spending, audit, health, education)? For what decision?
2. Where did you get them? How long did it take?
3. What went wrong: missing data, conflicting figures, scanned PDFs, out-of-date numbers?
4. Who in your organisation does this work, and how many hours a month?

## Pain and value (10 minutes)
5. If one source showed every figure with its page and flagged conflicts, what would that change for you?
6. Which of these would you use: quarterly evidence pack, bespoke brief, monthly digest, data licence, training? Why?
7. What would make you trust it? What would make you stop using it?

## Procurement and price (8 minutes)
8. How does your organisation buy research or data? Who approves, and what paperwork is needed?
9. What have you paid for similar work? Would KES [test price] for [product] be low, fair or high?
10. Would you take part in a paid pilot? What would success look like after three months?

## Close (2 minutes)
Thank them; offer the free platform link; agree whether a follow-up is welcome (by WhatsApp, at their choice).
