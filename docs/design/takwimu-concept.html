Siaya County · Own-source revenue · FY 2024/25

Two official numbers for Siaya's own revenue differ by more than two to one. Here is why.

The Controller of Budget and the county report different totals for the same year. Takwimu shows both, names the source of each, and explains what could cause the gap.

What to take from this: a gap like this usually means the two figures count different things, such as cash collected against revenue billed, or different reporting dates. Check the definition before the number.
