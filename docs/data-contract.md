# Data contract (version 1.7)

Applies to `data/history.json`, `data/conflicts.json`, `data/national.json` and `data/counties-cob.json`. Enforced by `scripts/validate-data.js` at build time, including the JSON Schemas in `schemas/` (also for `subcounties.json` and `county.json`).

## Observations (history.json)
Each year carries: fiscal year, figures, `report` (default source), optional `field_source` (a different report for one field), optional `derived` (fields computed rather than printed), `caveat`. Each report has title, URL and confidence (1 to 5, an explanation of source quality and extraction, not a probability of accuracy). Gaps are listed in `meta.gaps`, never filled.

Audit points may carry `source` (when the opinion comes from a compilation, not the audit report) and `caveat`.

## Page references (since 21 Nov 2026)
Every observation names the document it was read from and the page: each year's `provenance` maps every figure field to a reference; audit points, own-source revenue points and their alternates carry the reference directly. A reference has `source_id` (an id in `data/source-manifest.json`), `page` (PDF page, counted from the first page of the file), optional `printed_page` (only where at least three pages of the section show the same offset between PDF and printed numbers), `read` (`PDF text layer`, `OCR layer` or `RapidOCR`) and optional `page_note`. A figure that could not be found again has `page: null` and `page_note: "not located"`; a page is never guessed. In-year periods and supplementary documents carry `source_id`; their figures keep `pdf_page`. The validator fails if a figure has no reference, if `source_id` is not in the manifest, if the manifest document's link differs from the report or audit link the observation cites, if a null page lacks "not located", or if a reference exists for a gap. The CSV export carries `source_id`, `pdf_page`, `printed_page` and `page_note` on every row that has them; the Data page lists them per year.

## In-year figures (history.json `in_year`, since 21 Nov 2026)
Partial-year Controller of Budget figures (FY 2025/26 first quarter and first half). Each period names its report, section and confidence; each figure has measure, value, unit, PDF and printed page, how it was read and, for balances, `as_of`. `comparability` is fixed to "In-year, not comparable with full years". The validator fails if the in-year fiscal year also appears in `years`, or if an in-year conflict id is not in the register. Pages show these apart from the ten-year series, never on the same chart.

## Pending-bills roll-forward (history.json `pending_rollforward`, since 21 Nov 2026)
One row per fiscal year with the identity opening stock + new bills − payments − write-offs or adjustments = closing stock. Each term is either published (value in KSh million, `source_id` of that year's report, PDF page, printed page where known, how it was read, optional note and `parts`) or `{"status": "not published"}` with no value. A closing stock is never computed from the other terms, and a missing term is never filled. The validator checks that each published term cites the year's report and has a page, that `parts` add up, and that a "not published" term has no value. `scripts/test-reconciliation.js` tests the identity for every year where all five terms are published and, for every other year, that the gap is shown as "not published"; the smoke test checks the Insights table shows every row and every unpublished term.

## Other documents (history.json `supplementary`)
Figures for a year whose Controller of Budget report is missing (FY 2019/20), from other primary documents (county Budget Review and Outlook Paper, later CoB reports, Senate committee report quoting the audit). Listed with page numbers; never used to fill `years`. Disagreements with another source go in the register.

## Conflict register (conflicts.json)
Each case has: id, measure, unit, at least two sourced values (label, value, https URL), definitions, a labelled hypothesis, status (`open` or `resolved`), effect on the pages, and:
- `verification_state`: `resolved_by_source`, `unresolved_no_reviewer` or `reviewed`. A `reviewed` case must name `reviewer` and `reviewed_on`. No case is reviewed yet.
- `page_image_checked`: true only when the figure was checked against the page image, not only the extracted text. Currently false for all.
- `supersedes`: id of an earlier case this one restates, or null.
- `reread` (optional, since 21 Nov 2026): a two-method machine re-reading of the printed figures: date, the two methods, each place re-read with both readings, the arithmetic check and the outcome. A case is `resolved_by_source` by re-reading only when every printed place gives the same value by both methods and the value agrees with the printed amounts within 0.5 points; if the document itself prints two values, the case stays open. A re-read never sets `page_image_checked` (the validator fails if it does).

## National indicators (national.json, since 21 Nov 2026)
Eighteen World Bank World Development Indicators series for Kenya, 2010 to 2025, retrieved from the API on 21 Nov 2026 (fourteen first, then four external debt series from International Debt Statistics: DT.DOD.DECT.CD and DT.DOD.DPPG.CD in current US$, DT.DOD.DECT.GN.ZS in % of GNI, DT.TDS.DECT.EX.ZS in % of exports of goods, services and primary income) (`source_id: worldbank-wdi-api` in the manifest; a live API, so not hashed). Each indicator has code, name, unit, sector, the API's source note, original source, `api_url`, `last_updated`, retrieval date, a note on modelled or estimated data, `missing_years` and one value per year. The validator fails if years are not consecutive, if a null year is not in `missing_years` (or a listed year has a value), or if a percentage (other than gross enrolment) is outside 0 to 100. Gaps (central government debt, the IMF series that could not be reached, net enrolment, the redefined poverty line) are in `meta.gaps`. Stored values keep the World Bank unit; pages divide some by a scale for display (population in million people, remittances and external debt stocks in US$ billion), and `scripts/test-explore.js` fails the build if a display unit and its scale disagree or a download changes the raw value or unit. External debt is debt owed to non-residents; it is labelled with the World Bank's names and a note that it is not total public debt (tested), and central government debt stays in `meta.gaps`.

## 47-county tables (counties-cob.json, since 21 Nov 2026)
Four years (FY 2021/22 to FY 2024/25) of the Controller of Budget annual county reports, chapter 2: own-source revenue (Table 2.1 or 2.2), budget, spending and absorption (Table 2.5, 2.3 or 2.4), pending bills (Table 2.9, 2.5 or 2.6). The older tables have fewer columns: FY 2022/23 prints own-source revenue without FIF only (stored as `ordinary_osr_*`) and pending bills without subtotals; FY 2021/22 prints one undivided own-source revenue figure (stored as `total_osr_*`, with a note that FIF inclusion is not stated). A column a year's table does not print has status `not_in_table` for every county and the total, with a note; nothing is computed in its place. Every value carries `table`, `pdf_page`, `printed_page`, `read: "ocr"`, the raw printed string and the OCR confidence. A null value has `status` (`blank` or `doubtful`) and a `note`; a doubtful value keeps its raw reading. The `derived` block (percentages computed from amounts) is for reference and is never shown as a value. The validator checks the schema, that each year's `source_id`, link and SHA-256 match the manifest, that the 47 counties match `lib/counties.js`, that the printed page is the PDF page minus the year's offset, that the 47 rows sum to the printed total (within 0.5 KSh m), that row parts add up (recurrent plus development, ordinary plus FIF/A-i-A, Executive plus Assembly, or the four pending-bill parts where a table has no subtotals; identities with a `not_in_table` part are skipped), that a `not_in_table` column covers every row, that printed percentages agree with the printed amounts (within 1.5 points), and that Siaya's rows equal `history.json` within its rounding or the difference is in the register (`budget-2024-25`, `recurrent-absorption-2024-25`, `recurrent-absorption-2023-24`, `development-absorption-2023-24`, `pending-close-2025`, `pending-2024`, `budget-financing-osr-2024-25`, and for the older years `pending-2023` (Table 2.6, 873.37) and `pending-close-2022` (Table 2.6, 751.99)). The first two years were imported once by `scripts/import-staging.js`; FY 2022/23 and 2021/22 were parsed from 300 dpi RapidOCR boxes (angle classifier off) on 21 Nov 2026, after checking each PDF's SHA-256 against the manifest.

### Re-read cells (since 21 Nov 2026)
A percentage cell that was doubtful or not read carries `reread`: date, `method_a` and `method_b` (how, reading, confidence), the arithmetic `check`, `outcome` (`accepted: ...` or `not accepted: ...`), `first_pass` and `page_image_checked: false`. Method A is the RapidOCR recogniser alone on a 600 dpi greyscale crop of the cell with the table rules removed; method B is RapidOCR detector and recogniser on a 400 dpi crop, Otsu binarised and upscaled 2x. Both run with the 0/180-degree angle classifier off (it turned short numbers upside down in the first pass: 91 read as 16, 90 as 06). The crops come from the OCR word boxes and the table rules (`scripts/ocr/reread-cells.py`). A value is accepted only when both readings agree and are within 0.5 points of the printed amounts; it then keeps the first-pass reading in `ocr_reading`. Otherwise the value stays null with status `doubtful` (a cell with ink that neither method read reliably is doubtful, not blank) and a note. The validator checks that an accepted value equals both readings and the amounts within 0.5 points, and that a not-accepted one is null. Tesseract is not installed in the build environment, so the second reading is a second RapidOCR pipeline with different resolution, preprocessing and detection, not a second engine.

### Not yet verified (since 21 Nov 2026)
A doubtful cell whose re-read was not accepted is shown as "Not yet verified" with its note as the reason: in the county comparison (list, map label, table view, side by side), in every download (status `not yet verified`, note starting "Not yet verified: ", value and `raw_reading` empty) and in stories. Seven cells: FY 2024/25 printed total own-source revenue performance; development absorption for Makueni, Murang'a, Samburu and Trans Nzoia; Mombasa overall absorption; and FY 2021/22 Kilifi development absorption. They are listed in `lib/unverified.js`; the data file keeps the raw reading and both re-readings. `scripts/test-explore.js` fails if the list and the data disagree or if any view or download shows one of them as a number. A cell leaves the list only when a reviewer checks it against the page image.

## Central Bank of Kenya, derived measures, CRA, IBP and IEBC (since 21 Nov 2026)
- `data/cbk.json`: 31 CBK series (exchange rates, policy and market interest rates, commercial bank rates, public debt, government revenue, interest and expenditure, M2 and M3, GDP from KNBS, inflation, remittances, reserves), January 2010 to the latest month. Each series has name, unit, frequency, definition, the file's URL, SHA-256 and `source_id` (manifest), and the column. The validator checks domestic plus external equals total public debt every month, that every null month is listed in `gaps.null_cells`, and that each fiscal-year value is the June cumulative value. Pages show one published reading a year (December, or the fiscal-year total), named in each series' basis; monthly values are in the downloads.
- `data/national.json` gained 35 further World Bank indicators (53 in all, 2010 to 2025), same structure, with `raw_sha256` of the API response. Sectors: economy, money and banking, public finance, people, health, education, infrastructure, environment.
- `data/derived.json`: 24 measures computed from the published series, each with `derived: true`, a formula and its inputs; null where any input is missing. `scripts/test-derived.js` (in the build) recomputes 144 values from `cbk.json`. Pages label them derived and show the formula (Kenya dashboard, Analysis).
- `data/ngo.json`: the Commission on Revenue Allocation (a constitutional commission) FY 2026/27 recommendation: county allocations (Table 6.2), Fourth Basis weights (Table 6.1), vertical sharing (Table 5.1) and the Equalisation Fund (Table 5.3). Labelled as a recommendation, not the enacted act; the validator checks 47 counties, baseline plus Fourth Basis equals each total, the totals add to the published KSh 458,940 million, and the Equalisation Fund is 0.5 per cent of audited revenue. IBP Open Budget Survey (Kenya, 2006 to 2025) with IBP's requested citation kept and shown.
- `data/political.json`: IEBC registered voters 2022 for the 290 constituencies (they add to the file's 22,102,532; county totals are sums and marked derived) and candidates by position 2022. The IEBC states no reuse licence; the datasets say so. The annulled 8 August 2017 presidential results are left out (unsigned copy; county rows do not add to the national total), and the validator fails if they reappear. Place and institution level only; no individual is named or scored.
- Register cases with tests: `registered-voters-2022` (22,102,532 against 22,120,458), `deposit-rate-cbk-wdi` (CBK 10.83 against World Bank 11.98 per cent in 2024; up to 3.67 points in 2012), `candidates-2022-woman-member` (printed parts add to one less than the printed totals).
- Manifest: 31 new documents with SHA-256 and bytes; `md5_library` is required only for Parliament library files (other publishers give no checksum).

## Explore pages and downloads
`lib/explore.js` reads these files for /datasets/, /kenya/, /compare/, /trends/ and /stories/ and for the build's downloads in `public/data/datasets/` (one CSV and JSON per dataset plus `catalogue.json`). Views download exactly the rows they show. Comparisons do not order counties by value when any reading for that indicator and year is doubtful. Story numbers are computed from the data in `lib/stories.js`.

## Rules
- Original observations are never overwritten when a later report restates them; both stay in the register.
- Arithmetic agreement alone never upgrades a case to `reviewed`.
- Exports (`public/data/history.csv`, `history.json`, `conflicts.json`) are built from these same files.

## Not yet in place
Geography versioning, price-basis fields on every observation, and a reviewer workflow. (Source file checksums are in `data/source-manifest.json` since 21 Nov 2026. A document from a site that publishes no checksum is listed with `sha256: null` and `access: "downloaded <date>; not hashed: <reason>"`; one that could not be fetched has `access: "unavailable on <date>"`.) These need an owner decision on who reviews.
