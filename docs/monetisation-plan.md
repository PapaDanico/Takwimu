# Monetisation plan (draft, 21 Nov 2026)

Principle: public facts stay free; revenue comes from time-saving services built on the same verified data. Nothing paid changes a figure, a ranking or a caveat, and no buyer gets editorial influence.

## Customers, in order of likely revenue
1. Offices of MPs, governors, senators and MCAs (constituency and county planning, campaign-season research).
2. County treasuries and assemblies (budget committees, oversight).
3. NGOs, donors and researchers (programme design, baseline data).
4. Media and businesses (market entry, reporting).

## Products
| Product | What it is | Price idea (KSh) |
|---|---|---|
| Free platform | All dashboards, comparisons, downloads, stories | 0 |
| Constituency / county brief | Branded PDF: population, voters, allocations (NG-CDF, equitable share), budget execution, pending bills, services, trends, sources | 15,000 to 50,000 per brief |
| Annual office subscription | Updated briefs each quarter, alerts when new reports land, custom comparisons | 150,000 to 500,000 a year |
| Data support | Custom tables, verification of figures used in speeches and manifestos, training for staff | Day rate |
| Institutional licence | Bulk data with documentation for NGOs, donors, media | Negotiated |

## Safeguards (protect credibility, which is the asset)
- Strictly non-partisan: the same products are offered to every party and independent candidate; no scoring or rating of named individuals.
- Paid work is never published as Takwimu analysis unless it meets the public method, and client names are not used to imply endorsement.
- Corrections policy applies to paid outputs as to the free site.
- Legal review of terms of service, data licences of third-party sources, and the Data Protection Act before charging.

## First steps
1. Finish constituency-level data (IEBC voters, census population, NG-CDF and CRA allocations) now being staged.
2. Build one sample constituency brief and one county brief from it.
3. Test with three offices across different parties; refine price and contents.
4. Add a simple "Request a brief" contact route (needs the private contact address still outstanding).
