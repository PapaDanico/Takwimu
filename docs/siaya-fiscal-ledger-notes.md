# Siaya fiscal reconciliation ledger: notes (21 Nov 2026)

Files: `data/siaya-fiscal-ledger.json`, `schemas/siaya-fiscal-ledger.schema.json`, `scripts/test-fiscal-ledger.js` (run `node scripts/test-fiscal-ledger.js`; part of npm run validate and npm run build). Page: /counties/siaya/ledger/; dataset siaya-fiscal-ledger). Research programme sections 1, 3A, 4 (stages 3 to 5), 6 and 8. Not yet reviewed by a specialist; every discrepancy is `open`.

## What it holds
- 16 measures, FY 2013/14 to FY 2025/26: budget total, recurrent and development (with `budget_version`), equitable share budgeted and received, conditional grants or additional allocations budgeted and received, own-source revenue target, total, ordinary and FIF/A-i-A, expenditure total, recurrent and development, pending bills at 30 June, and wage bill.
- 299 values side by side: 148 reused from `data/history.json` and `data/counties-cob.json`, carrying their provenance and a `reused_from` path (the test checks that they match exactly), and 151 new readings with table or section, PDF page, printed page, read method, original text and confidence.
- New readings come from county Q4 budget implementation reports (GOV-02: FY 2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26; Q1 FY 2025/26 for the original budget), the county BROP for FY 2024/25 (GOV-03) and FY 2019/20, the CoB annual county chapters (equitable share, grants, wage bill), Auditor-General reports for FY 2013/14 and FY 2020/21 to 2023/24, and the Senate CPAC report for FY 2019/20.
- Read methods: PDF text layer, or tesseract at 300 dpi where the scanned CoB text layer is garbled. Tesseract corrected two text-layer misreads: the equitable share received in FY 2023/24 is 6.68bn, not 5.68bn (re-checked at 600 dpi on 21 Nov 2026 against Table 3.258; recorded as resolved case equitable-share-received-2023-24 in data/conflicts.json), and in FY 2024/25 it is 7.88bn, not "7.ti8".
- 142 discrepancy cases. A case is any pair of values for the same measure and year that differ by more than 1% of the smaller value. Each case uses the programme's schema, and `possible_basis_mismatch` is generated from scope, basis, version, stock point and publisher. Original and supplementary budgets are not compared with each other.
- The completeness matrix lists, for each measure and year, the number of values, the number of sources, the source kinds and whether the cell is missing, with a reason.

## Largest open cases (for review)
- **OSR FY 2024/25:** CoB gives 436.68m (ordinary 236.84 + FIF 199.84). The county BROP and Q4 report give 757.57m (A-i-A 521.07m). The county FY 2025/26 Q4 report quotes 712.82m. This is the programme's KNA example: the difference is in A-i-A/FIF coverage.
- **Expenditure FY 2024/25:** CoB gives 7,748.65m. The BROP gives 8,939.55m (recurrent 6,176.65m, which is 101.7% of budget). The Q4 report gives 6,820.23m. Within the BROP, development spending is 2,762.90m in Table 9 but 2,674.33m in the text.
- **Equitable share received FY 2024/25:** CoB 7.88bn, county Q4 7,882.51m, BROP 7,301.47m.
- **Pending bills:** totals for the same date vary widely. FY 2023/24 has values from 303.91m (CoB, which excludes the year's new bills) up to 1,466.05m (county management statement in the audited accounts). FY 2024/25 has 545.95m (Table 3.457, Executive), 1.12bn (CoB text), 1,890m (CoB cross-county Table 2.9) and 1,473.85m (county opening stock at 1 July 2025). FY 2020/21 has 1,060.92m (CoB) against 601.74m (AG, Annexure 2) and 918.60m (management).
- **Wage bill:** in FY 2016/17 to 2018/19, the CoB key-issue paragraphs quote a "wage bill" (1.12bn, 1.58bn and 1.98bn) that is far below personnel emoluments spent (2.31bn, 2.40bn and 2.75bn). The basis of the smaller figure is not defined. For FY 2024/25, the CoB gives 2.99bn against 3.40bn (BROP) and 3.34bn (county, a year later).
- **OSR FY 2020/21:** CoB gives 332.88m. A chart in the county FY 2025/26 Q4 report gives 434.36m, which equals the FY 2021/22 figure; this may be a copy error in the chart.

## Gaps and not used
- FY 2013/14: the CoB report for that year has not been read. FY 2019/20: no CoB annual report was found. FY 2025/26: only county and CoB in-year reports are held. For FY 2014/15 only a development pending-bills figure was published, so there is no total. Ordinary/FIF splits start in FY 2022/23.
- IBP CBTS 2021/2022 is not used because it states no fiscal totals. The county BROP FY 2020/21 copy is held locally but is not in the source library, so it is not cited. CoB in-year FY 2025/26 figures other than the approved budget are partial-year and are not used.
- No named individuals are recorded. The signatories printed in the county reports are left out.

## Rebuilding
The ledger was generated by a one-off script, using `computeDiscrepancies` and `computeCompleteness` exported from the test, so the stored cases equal what the test recomputes. To add a value, append an observation with its page and printed text, then regenerate the discrepancies and completeness with those functions.
