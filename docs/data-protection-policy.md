# Data protection by design: no engagement with the ODPC (owner policy, 21 Nov 2026)

Policy for every Danico Ventures platform: build and run so that nothing obliges us to register with, notify or report to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). Benchmarked on 21 Nov 2026 against the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 (Legal Notice 265 of 2021), as published at odpc.go.ke. This is a design rule, not legal advice; an advocate should confirm the business-level points.

## What would bring us into contact with the ODPC, and how the platform avoids it

| Trigger | Source | How Takwimu avoids it | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registration as a data controller or processor | Act s.18-19; Regulations r.13 | The site processes no personal data, so it creates no registration duty. For the business, r.13 exempts a controller with annual turnover or revenue below KSh 5 million **and** fewer than 10 employees, unless it processes for a Third Schedule purpose | No forms, accounts, analytics, cookies or trackers (smoke test) |
| Third Schedule purposes (registration regardless of size) | Regulations, Third Schedule: 1 canvassing political support among the electorate; 10 businesses wholly or mainly in direct marketing (and others: CCTV, gambling, education institutions, health, hospitality, property, financial services, telecoms, transport, genetic data) | Briefs use published aggregate statistics only; no voter or resident data; no campaign canvassing, voter targeting or direct-marketing work (stated on /briefs/request/) | Page text; monetisation safeguards |
| Breach notification | Act s.43 | No personal data stored on the platform, so there is nothing to breach | As above |
| Data protection impact assessment | Act s.31 | No high-risk processing | As above |
| Data subject complaints | Act s.56 | Nothing is collected about visitors. The site names no individual officials; political data is counts by office and constituency only | Data files are aggregate (checked 21 Nov 2026) |

## The one remaining personal-data touchpoint
Brief requests arrive by WhatsApp (wa.me/message/GPPIKMENXJVWO1). Those chats are processed by Danico Ventures, not by the site. To stay within the r.13 exemption: keep turnover and staff below the thresholds or register when either is crossed; use chat details only to reply; never build contact lists or send marketing; delete chats when the request closes. If the business grows past the thresholds, registration becomes mandatory; there is no design that avoids it at that size.

## Rules for future features (all platforms)
1. No forms, sign-ups, accounts, comments, newsletters, analytics, cookies, pixels or third-party trackers.
2. No data about identifiable individuals, including named officials, voters or residents; publish aggregates only.
3. Contact happens off-platform (WhatsApp or similar), started by the visitor.
4. No service whose purpose is political canvassing, voter targeting or direct marketing.
5. Any change that breaks rules 1-4 needs the owner's written decision first.
