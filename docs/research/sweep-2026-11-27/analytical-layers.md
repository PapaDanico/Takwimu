# New analytical layers for Siaya (proposal)

The site already has these layers (`data/siaya-analysis.json`): A1 real per-capita expenditure (4 values, 12 gaps), A2 transfer dependency, A3 execution rate, A4 change in pending-bills stock, A5 audit-finding categories, A6 education spend vs ECDE, A7 climate allocation vs PCRA, A8 FIF share of OSR.

The layers below are new or substantially extended. All follow the repo rule: arithmetic on staged values, with numerator and denominator from the same document, and no gap filled. The legal thresholds come from the PFM Act 2012 s.107 and PFM (County Governments) Regulations 2015 reg. 25. These should be cited from the primary text when staged; I have not fetched them in this sweep.

Ranked by value divided by effort. The top 5 come first.

## Top 5 (recommended to build first)

1. **L1 wage-bill vs 35%.** The data is mostly held, the legal rule is clear, and audience interest is high. The OAG ratios are already in findings.
2. **L2 development share vs 30%.** The new PBBs give the budget-version split for 11 years.
3. **L3 OSR on three bases (real, per capita, vs target).** The audited 12-year RoR table makes this the first 12-year OSR series with an audited source, and it directly explains the FY2024/25 dispute.
4. **L4 departmental absorption.** This is the main value of the new PBB set; it needs a department crosswalk.
5. **L6 pending-bills ageing and burden.** It ties the 42 open cases to the OAG ageing annexes and the new CFSP annexes, and shows the OAG "liabilities vs 20%" test.

Each needs a schema, a test (units, same-document numerator and denominator), and a table view, in line with the chart rule.
