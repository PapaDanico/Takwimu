## Findings while reading

- KIPPRA's record "Budget Review and Outlook Paper 2023" is the **September 2024** paper (it reviews FY 2023/24; cover and text). The September 2023 paper (FY 2022/23) is not in the repository found; FY 2022/23 is a listed gap. The sweep's "CBROP 2024 missing" is therefore wrong: CBROP 2024 is held, CBROP 2023 is missing.
- The FY 2014/15 and 2015/16 programme-based budgets are supplementary budgets that also print the original ("gross") estimates; FY 2016/17 and 2019/20 likewise. Versions are kept apart in the data.
- Documents that do not add up (flagged, never corrected): PBB 2014/15 (development Total row 2,000,000 above its parts: Agriculture 225,938,791 in Table 3 against 223,938,791 in Tables 4 and 5; compensation Total 20,000,000 above its parts); PBB 2025/26 (PE and O&M Total rows differ from their parts by 433,000 each, in opposite directions); CBROP 2021 (recurrent plus development actual does not equal total actual for seven departments; whole county 16,200,935).
- CBROP of September 2024, Table 12: every department except the County Assembly shows the same 95.4 per cent PE absorption, so the department PE actuals look allocated pro rata. They are stored as printed and not used in analysis.
- CBROP 2019 (FY 2018/19) prints two conditional-grant budgets: KSh 844,302,787 "conditional allocations" in the resource basket (PDF p. 9) and 947,884,880 in Tables 4 and 5 (PDF p. 11). It also prints own-source revenue of 189,668,022 (Table 4) and 173,996,994 (text, PDF p. 11). Both readings are stored (`siaya-county-series.json`).
- CBROP FY 2019/20 prints own-source revenue as 184,249,749 (Table 3, PDF p. 13) and 184,249,748 (PDF p. 15).
- The Auditor-General's FY 2024/25 Receiver of Revenue report restates FY 2023/24 own-source revenue as 610,405,666.75 (Table 1) against 610,364,012 in the FY 2023/24 report and statement; FY 2022/23 is 501,460,021 in both tables against 501,561,621 in the audited comparative column. The tables are County Treasury figures in the management discussion (Other Information, outside the audit opinion).
- FY 2024/25 on three bases, one audited report: cash receipts 757,633,212.40 (statement of cash flows, PDF p. 29), accrual revenue 948,124,858 (statement of financial performance, PDF p. 26), target 1,044,061,306. The county's 946.9m in the press is close to the accrual total. Cash receipts plus the stated receivables (189,260,228) fall 1,231,417.60 short of the accrual total; recorded, not forced.
