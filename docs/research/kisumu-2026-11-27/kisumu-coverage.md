## 4. Scope notes for ingest

- **Name mapping**: KIPPRA calls the county "Kisumu City County". CoB, OAG and the county use "County Government of Kisumu". All map to `lib/counties.js` code 42, slug `kisumu`.
- **City of Kisumu**: the City Board and its accounts are audited separately and are a department in CoB budget-by-department tables ("City of Kisumu" row). Label the scope explicitly and never add it to county totals twice.
- **OSR basis**: from FY2023/24, CoB reports OSR including FIF/A-i-A (FY2024/25: ordinary 803.36m plus FIF 1,659.67m = 2,463.03m). Show the ordinary and FIF parts separately. The FY2024/25 "rise" is mostly FIF (a definitional case, as with Siaya).
- **Pending bills**: CoB 30 June 2025 = 1,350.5m (counties-cob.json). The county "Pending bills as at 30 June 2025" (scan, 1 page) is held for comparison.
- **Personal data**: budget cover pages and OAG reports name office holders. Use roles only. Withhold contractor names from project tables.
