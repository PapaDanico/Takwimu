## Conflicts (show side by side)

- **Siaya expenditure:** CBROP (C p.21, Table 10) gives 9,605.52 against the same budget, which is 71.80 % absorption (PE 4,188.77; O&M 2,635.17; development 2,781.58, 46.14 %). CoB gives 7,504.73 (56.10 %).
- **Siaya OSR:** CBROP (C p.5) "Local revenue collection was Kshs. 862,934,946.65 against a target of Kshs. 3,252,420,825". CoB gives 876.14.
- **Siaya Q4 report contradicts itself:** its text says "The County spent Kshs.7.18 billion" (B p.6), but its components and table sum to 7,504.73. Its trade payables (1,276,882,801.13, pp.15-16) match CoB.
- **Siaya budget figure:** CoB H1 gives the approved budget as 12.79 bn; the annual 13.38 bn is the supplementary budget.
- **Economic Survey 2026 (gap D):** T5.13 prints Siaya FY2025/26 own-source revenue as 3,114.92. That is close to the total OSR target (3,252.42) and far from the actual (876.14). Treat it as a probable source error and record it in the source-error register once reviewed.

Siaya Q3 (D) gives context only: 4.52 bn spent (p.7); OSR 607 m, "20 per cent of the annual target" (p.5); compensation 2.93 bn (p.9).
