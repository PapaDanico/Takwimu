## FY2025/26: Siaya OSR and the Economic Survey

- The Economic Survey 2026 figure of 3,114.92 m is the Siaya PBB 2025/26 OSR budget: 2,036,235,308 ordinary plus 1,078,685,515 SHA A-in-A (PBB p.13). It is not collections, so record it as a source error in the Economic Survey.
- The county reports actual collections of 862,934,946.65 = 272,116,888.45 ordinary + 590,818,058.20 A-in-A, against a target of 3,252,420,825 (S26 pp.5, 15).
- The same CBROP gives 858,616,248 in Tables 8/9 (pp.17, 19), so S26 disagrees with itself.
- CoB reports 876.14 m (p.38).
